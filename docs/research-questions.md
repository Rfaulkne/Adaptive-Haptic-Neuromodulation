# Research Questions

## Primary question

**Can remote mechanical haptic stimulation measurably and reproducibly modulate experimentally induced pain?**

## Secondary questions

1. Which stimulus parameters matter most?
   - Frequency
   - Amplitude
   - Rhythm
   - Spatial pattern
   - Apparent motion
   - Body location
   - Predictability / irregularity

2. Are spatially moving or patterned stimuli more effective than continuous vibration?

3. Does the effect persist after stimulation stops?

4. Can the effect be separated from attention or distraction?

5. How much response variability is attributable to:
   - Participant
   - Session
   - Stimulus type
   - Stimulation intensity
   - Body location

6. Do participants show stable individual response profiles across sessions?

## Mechanistic questions

- Is the effect consistent with local sensory gating, heterotopic modulation, descending pain inhibition, attentional competition, or some combination?
- Can objective or semi-objective measures of nociceptive processing complement subjective pain ratings?
- Does stronger perceived haptic intensity necessarily produce stronger analgesia?

## Research sequence

1. **Establish the effect**
2. **Characterize dose / stimulus dependence**
3. **Test mechanism**
4. **Quantify individual variability**
5. **Only then investigate personalization**
6. **Only then investigate sensing and closed-loop timing**

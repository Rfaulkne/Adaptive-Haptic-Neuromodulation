# Design and Wearable Precedents

These are useful references for product architecture, sensing integration, and long-term wearability rather than direct proof of pain modulation.

## Empatica EmbracePlus
Research wearable integrating EDA, PPG, skin temperature, accelerometry, and gyroscope data.

https://www.empatica.com/embraceplus

**Relevant lesson:** multiple physiological signals can be collected continuously in a single research-grade wearable.

## Oura Ring
Consumer wearable emphasizing longitudinal baseline trends in HR, HRV, temperature, sleep, respiration, and activity.

https://ouraring.com/

**Relevant lesson:** complex physiology can be translated into an unobtrusive object and interpreted relative to the individual's own baseline.

## WHOOP
Continuous cardiovascular, temperature, respiratory and activity monitoring with emphasis on personal baseline and longitudinal change.

https://www.whoop.com/

**Relevant lesson:** state estimation may be more useful as deviation from an individual's norm than as a universal threshold.

## BioButton / BioIntelliSense
Body-worn continuous physiological monitoring in a small adhesive / clip-style format.

https://www.biointellisense.com/

**Relevant lesson:** monitoring can disappear into the body rather than requiring a conventional smartwatch interaction model.

## VitalPatch
Adhesive physiological sensor platform including ECG, respiration, activity, posture and related measurements.

https://vitalconnect.com/

**Relevant lesson:** body placement can be optimized for signal quality when user interaction is not required.

## Hexoskin / Astroskin
Physiological sensing integrated into garments.

https://www.hexoskin.com/

**Relevant lesson:** distributed sensing and stimulation could eventually migrate into clothing rather than remain a single wrist or arm module.

## Movesense
Programmable wearable ECG / motion platform with APIs and custom development support.

https://www.movesense.com/

**Relevant lesson:** early research prototypes do not need to reinvent every physiological sensor.

## Nix Hydration Biosensor
Reusable electronics combined with a disposable body interface.

https://nixbiosensors.com/

**Relevant lesson:** modular separation of electronics from skin-contact components may be useful for experimental haptic modules.

## Gatorade Gx Sweat Patch / Rogers group
Research-to-product example of soft skin-mounted sensing translated into an accessible consumer form.

**Relevant lesson:** body-integrated sensing can progress from unconventional laboratory technology to a simple, product-centered interaction model.
